# Muse Skill Collection

The Muse Skill Collection: 293 ready-to-install skills for Muse

New to Muse skills? A skill is a small folder of plain-English instructions that teaches your Muse a new job: designing pages, writing marketing copy, reviewing code, planning research, making art, running a store, and much more. Install one in seconds, and your Muse can do that job whenever you ask.

What is inside: 10 themed packs, 293 skills in total.
- Design (23)
- Marketing (60)
- Knowledge and formats (35)
- Coding, engineering, testing and security (44)
- Muse adaptations (6)
- Art, image, video and audio (32)
- Social media and content (34)
- Agent autonomy (28)
- Business and finance (14)
- Data, home, developer and productivity (17)

How to install: download the packs you want from the release page, unzip, and drop the skill folders into your Muse custom skills folder. Done. Every pack includes a plain-English install guide written for Muse itself, so you can simply ask your Muse to install them for you.

Free, forever. Every skill keeps its original author credit and license in the attribution file included in each pack. Most are MIT or Apache-2.0; a few carry share-alike or non-commercial terms, all clearly flagged before you download.

Made for readers of the advanced Muse guide, and free for everyone.

All credit for the skills belongs to their original authors; the collection claims only the selection, conversion, adaptation notes, and packaging.
